## 1. What v0.7.0 is

When `v0.7.0` is tagged and `release.yml` is green:

- **The pump record has its own connection.** Settings → "Pumpe-historik" takes a Nightscout address and a read-only token (or API secret), separate from the space's glucose source, which stays what it is (Dexcom Share for the owner). The credential goes through the existing `source-connect` function to Key Vault as a version of `pump-<spaceId>`; the database keeps `{ type, secretRef, connectedBy }`. The card shows the status and the newest pump record with its age, because Tandem's upload lags by up to an hour.
- **The poller reads the boluses.** A new timer every 15 minutes reads `GET /api/v1/treatments.json` filtered on `eventType=Combo Bolus` (what tconnectsync writes) for the last 24 hours, or back to the newest stored bolus minus 3 hours if that is earlier, at most 14 days (the first run). It keeps the time, the delivered units, the kind (standard, extended, automatic) and the calculator's carbs; it drops everything else in the record (`glucose`, `enteredBy`, `utcOffset`, `_id`). `pump_event_id` is the idempotency key: re-polling adds nothing. The adapter only reads; `treatments.json` joins the allow-list with `GET` and nothing else.
- **Every bolus is on the day curve, with time and units.** Standard and extended boluses are the existing terracotta triangle; automatic ones (Control-IQ's own) are a smaller hollow marker in the text colour, never attached to a meal. On desktop a manual triangle carries its units as a small label; on the phone a tap on a marker shows "Bolus 11:58 · 2,4 E". Every marker's accessible name has time and units. The curve also says how far the pump record reaches ("Pumpe til 11:58"), so the hour of lag is never read as "no bolus".
- **A meal gets its bolus without a tap.** The nearest standard or extended bolus from 45 minutes before to 30 minutes after a meal's time becomes its `bolusTs` (and its units, shown on meal detail next to the time). "Bolus før måltid" and the week's lead figures then work as they did with the tap. One bolus belongs to at most one meal and one meal to at most one bolus.
- **Pump carbs** (decision 6, the one the owner should look at): the calculator's carbs are kept on the bolus. When the bolus belongs to a logged meal, meal detail shows them next to the logged total ("Pumpen: 45 g"). When no meal is logged in the window two hours after the bolus, the poller creates an unverified meal from them, like the Clarity import's carb entries: one manual line "Kulhydrater fra pumpen", marked "Fra pumpen · ikke tjekket". A carer ticks it ("Det passer") or edits it, and it becomes an ordinary meal. If someone logs the meal later, the logged meal takes the bolus and an untouched pump meal is removed; a pump meal a carer deletes is never made again.
- **Nothing new for a viewer or simpleMode, and nothing to a model.** The pump route refuses a viewer and a simpleMode session in the API; pump events, including every meal change the pump causes, travel in the glucose group; meal and history responses carry units, pump carbs and a pump-filled `bolusTs` only to owners and carers outside simpleMode, enforced in the API; untouched pump meals are left out of the viewer's day, simpleMode and every AI input. The model still gets the lead minutes and nothing else from the pump.
- **The hard rule is rewritten, not loosened by accident.** "No insulin dosing anywhere" becomes: Frejsi never computes, suggests or lets anyone enter insulin; it may show what the pump delivered, as pump history. Every statement of the rule, the gate agents, and the dosing-vocabulary test change together with the first schema that stores units (task 1).

## 3. Prisma schema (task 1, migration `0006_pump`)

- `PumpBolus`: `id` cuid, `spaceId`, `sourceId NVarChar(100)` (Nightscout's `pump_event_id`), `ts DateTime`, `units Decimal(5,2)`, `kind NVarChar(20)`, `carbs Int?` (grams typed into the pump's calculator; null when 0 or absent), `mealDismissed Boolean @default(false)` (a carer deleted the pump meal made from it, so none is made again), `createdAt`; relation to `Space` with `onDelete: Cascade` like `Reading`; `@@unique([spaceId, sourceId])`, `@@index([spaceId, ts])`. Back-relation `Space.pumpBoluses`.
- `Space.pumpSource NVarChar(Max)?` (JSON `PumpSourceConfig`), `Space.pumpStatus String @default("not-configured")`, `Space.pumpFailures Int @default(0)`, `Space.lastPumpAt DateTime?` (the newest stored bolus's `ts`).
- `Meal.pumpBolusId String?` with a relation to `PumpBolus` (`onDelete: NoAction, onUpdate: NoAction`, like the other meal relations, so SQL Server sees no second cascade path), `@@index([pumpBolusId])`; `Meal.source NVarChar(20)?` (`"pump"` for a meal the poller made from pump carbs and nobody has ticked or edited yet; null for every other meal).
- Not `@unique` on `Meal.pumpBolusId`: SQL Server treats NULLs as equal in a unique index, and a filtered index would break `prisma:check`. One bolus per meal is kept by the matcher inside a **Serializable** transaction, retried once on a serialization failure, and the integration test races a meal write against a pump cycle.
- `units` comes back as `Prisma.Decimal`; every reader converts with `.toNumber()` before the `number` schemas.
- `prisma:check` diffs `feel.prisma → schema.prisma` against `0006_pump/migration.sql`; `feel.prisma` is the 0005 schema frozen.

## 4. Zod contracts (task 1)

- `PumpBolusKind = enum(standard, extended, automatic, other)`. Only `standard` and `extended` ever attach to a meal or make a pump meal.
- `PumpBolus` (adapter output) `{ sourceId ≤ 100, ts, units: number 0–50 with at most two decimals, kind, carbs?: int 1–1000 }`.
- `PumpSourceConfig = strictObject({ type: literal("nightscout"), secretRef, connectedBy: userId })`. The address and the token are in Key Vault only.
- `ConnectPumpRequest = strictObject({ purpose: literal("pump"), type: literal("nightscout"), spaceId, url, token | apiSecret })` (the `NightscoutUrl` and one-of rules of `ConnectSourceRequest`; `NightscoutUrl` is exported from `schemas/glucose.ts` for it); the `source-connect` body is parsed as `ConnectPumpRequest` first and `ConnectSourceRequest` second, and both are strict, so a body with `purpose` can never be read as a glucose connect and overwrite `glucose-<spaceId>`. `ConnectPumpResult = { result: "ok", secretRef, recentBoluses? } | { result: "bad-credentials" } | { result: "unreachable" }`. `ConnectPumpBody` is what the Settings form posts (no `spaceId`, no `purpose`).
- `PumpBolusView { id, ts, units, kind, carbs?, mealId? }`; `PumpHistoryView { boluses: PumpBolusView[], reach?: ts (= lastPumpAt), status: SourceStatus }`; `PumpRangeQuery { from, to }` (≤ 8 days); `PumpSettingsView { status, since?, lastPumpAt?, configured: boolean }`.
- `MealView` gains `bolusUnits?: number`, `pumpCarbs?: int`, `pumpBolusId?`, `fromPump?: true`; `UpdateMealRequest` gains `adopt?: literal(true)`; `DayHistoryMeal` and the meal-curve view gain `pumpBolusId?` and `fromPump?` (the curve uses `pumpBolusId` to draw a meal's bolus once). `DayMealView` (the viewer's day) gains nothing.
- Events, appended after `symptom.deleted`: `pump.imported { count, from, to }` (one per space per cycle that stored anything; counts and the range only), `pump.meals { updated: mealId[], created: mealId[], removed: mealId[] }` (every meal change the pump causes: a link set or cleared, a pump meal made or removed, from the cycle or as a side effect of a person's meal write) and `pump.status { status, since }`. All three are added to `GLUCOSE_EVENT_TYPES`, so a viewer's socket and event page never carry them and a viewer cannot tell when pump data arrives. The person's own meal write still emits its `meal.created`/`meal.updated`/`meal.deleted` for that meal only, as today; a pump meal never emits `meal.created` (it carries `totalCarbs` and goes to viewers).
- New `ApiErrorCode`s `pump-not-configured` and `pump-meal` with `da`/`en` text in `web/messages/{da,en}/errors.json`.
- The dosing test walks every new schema. The keys are `units`, `kind`, `carbs`, `bolusUnits`, `pumpCarbs`, `pumpBolusId`, `fromPump`; the enum values are `standard`, `extended`, `automatic`, `other`. None is on `DOSING_WORDS` as matched (word-bounded, so `bolusUnits` is not `bolus`), and a new assertion fails if any schema key or enum value matches `SUGGESTION_WORDS` (§7).

## 5. The pump record from Nightscout (task 2)

- **Endpoint.** `ENDPOINTS.treatments = { method: "GET", path: "/api/v1/treatments.json" }`. `READ_ONLY_ENDPOINTS` grows by exactly this pair; the transport test asserts `POST`, `PUT` and `DELETE` on the same path are refused before `fetch`, and the lint boundary keeps the path inside `shared/src/glucose/nightscout/`.
- **Query.** `find[created_at][$gte]=<iso>&find[eventType]=Combo Bolus&count=<n>`, `n = 200` for a normal window and `1000` for the first; when a page comes back full, the next page asks `find[created_at][$lt]=<oldest seen>`, at most five pages, and a sixth full page is logged as `pump.page-cap` and picked up by the next cycle. Temp Basal (~400 a day) and Sleep are never fetched.
- **Window.** `since = max(now − 14 d, min(now − 24 h, lastPumpAt − 3 h))`. Tandem's upload lags by up to an hour and can arrive in a burst after the phone was offline; reading a full day every cycle plus three hours behind the newest stored bolus catches late records without a cursor that can skip.
- **Mapping.** `ts = Date.parse(created_at)` (UTC; `utcOffset` ignored), `units = insulin`, `carbs = carbs` when > 0, `sourceId = pump_event_id`. `notes`: `Standard Bolus` and `BLE Standard Bolus` → `standard`, `Extended Bolus` → `extended`, `Automatic Bolus` → `automatic`, anything else → `other`. A record without `pump_event_id`, with an unparseable time, `insulin` outside 0–50 or a non-number is dropped and counted (`pump.dropped` with the count). `glucose` and every other field are never read beyond the parse (`TreatmentEntry` picks the five fields; a test asserts the output has no other key).
- **`PumpSource`** (`shared/src/pump/types.ts`): `fetchBolusesSince(since): Promise<PumpBolus[]>` and `probe(): Promise<ProbeResult>`. `NightscoutPumpSource` implements it beside `NightscoutSource`, on the same transport and error type. Another pump route later (a file import, another uploader) is another implementation.
- **Cycle** (`runPumpCycle`, `shared/src/pump/poll-cycle.ts`): every space with a `pumpSource`, four at a time, one failure never stops the others. Per space: build the source from `pump-<spaceId>/<version>`; fetch; then commit per day of records (a 14-day first run is up to a few thousand rows on Basic SQL, too much for one interactive transaction; each commit sets an explicit `timeout`): insert the boluses whose `(spaceId, sourceId)` is new (find the existing ids, `createMany` the rest; SQL Server has no `skipDuplicates`, and a unique violation from a concurrent run is caught and the day re-read), update `lastPumpAt`, run the matcher over that day's window and write only `diffLinks`' differences, create the pump meals that are due, and emit `pump.imported` and `pump.meals` with the cycle's totals at the end. Three consecutive failures set `pumpStatus = disconnected` and emit `pump.status`; the first success sets it back. Timer `0 */15 * * * *` with 0–20 s jitter.
- **Connect.** `source-connect` with `purpose: "pump"` probes `verifyauth`, then reads a day of treatments on the same source and answers `recentBoluses`; on `ok` it writes the credential as a new version of `pump-<spaceId>` and answers the reference. Zero boluses in a day with a working token is the form's warning (tconnectsync not running, or the wrong site).

## 6. Matching boluses and meals (task 1 the rule, tasks 2 and 5 the callers)

`matchBoluses(meals, boluses)` in `shared/src/pump/match.ts`, pure and deterministic:

- Candidates: boluses of kind `standard` or `extended`; meals that are not untouched pump meals (`source = "pump"` meals keep the bolus they were made from and are never matched to another).
- A pair is possible when `meal.ts − 45 min ≤ bolus.ts ≤ meal.ts + 30 min`. Pairs are taken greedily by the smallest distance; on a tie the bolus before the meal wins, then the earlier meal. Each meal gets at most one bolus, each bolus at most one meal.
- The result for the window replaces the stored links: a meal whose link changes gets `pumpBolusId` and `bolusTs = bolus.ts`, or both cleared when it loses its bolus. A meal with a `bolusTs` from the old tap and no pump match keeps its `bolusTs`; a pump match replaces it.
- `diffLinks(current, matched)` (pure, beside the matcher) turns the stored links and the match into the writes: links to set, links to clear, pump meals to remove. Both callers use it.
- Callers: the pump cycle after storing (window: the new boluses' range widened by the 45/30 minutes), and the web meal service on create, on a time change, and on delete (window: around the meal's old and new time). Both write in one Serializable transaction with their own change and emit `pump.meals` for every other meal the match changed.
- **Pump meals.** A `standard` or `extended` bolus with carbs, no meal and `ts ≤ now − 2 h` gets a meal in the pump cycle: `ts = bolus.ts`, `byUserId = connectedBy`, one manual item named by `pump.meal.item` in that user's locale ("Kulhydrater fra pumpen"/"Carbs from the pump") with the bolus's carbs, `note` empty, `symbol: unknown`, `source: "pump"`, `pumpBolusId` and `bolusTs` from the bolus. When a person later logs or moves a meal into that bolus's window, the matcher gives the bolus to the person's meal, and the pump meal, still `source: "pump"`, is deleted in the same transaction and listed in `pump.meals.removed`. A pump meal an owner or carer deletes by hand sets `PumpBolus.mealDismissed`, so the cycle never makes it again. "Det passer" (`PATCH meals/[id]` with `{ adopt: true }`) or any edit by an owner or carer sets `source` to null: from then on it is an ordinary meal, matched like any other.
- Lead minutes (`bolusLeadMinutes`) are undefined for a meal whose `source` is `pump`: its time is the bolus time, so its lead would always be zero.

## 7. The hard rule, restated (task 1)

The owner's decision of 2026-10-18 (#216), in the words every rule file gets:

> **No insulin dosing.** Frejsi never computes, suggests or lets anyone enter insulin: no input field, no calculation, no ratio, no correction, no "you should", not in code, AI output schemas, prompts or copy. The one exception is **pump history**: the time and delivered units of a bolus the pump recorded, imported read-only, shown to owners and carers outside simpleMode, never to a viewer, never to an AI model. Showing what the pump delivered is allowed; setting or advising a dose is not.

Where it changes: `CLAUDE.md` (Hard rules), `AGENTS.md` (today it only points at `CLAUDE.md`; it gains one line naming the pump-history exception and pointing there), `.github/copilot-instructions.md` (item 1), the spec's §0 non-goals (the dosing bullet and "No pump data in v1" amended, dated), §2 (the `meals.bolusTs` row: from the pump record, no longer tapped by a carer; the `pump_boluses` row) and §5 (AI rules unchanged, the exception named), `.claude/agents/spec-guardian.md` (rule 1: what passes, what fails; `bolusTs` and pump-history units allowed, any input, calculation, ratio, correction or advice fails; the `insulin` field name allowed inside `shared/src/glucose/nightscout/` and `fixtures/nightscout/`, where it is Nightscout's own key, and nowhere else), `.claude/agents/copy-reviewer.md` (rule 5: pump units allowed only in `pump.json`, `pumpMeal.json` and `pumpSettings.json`, never advice; the Nightscout fixtures' `insulin` key allowed), `.claude/agents/implementer.md`, `.claude/agents/triage.md` (a pump-history request is no longer out of scope; dosing advice still is), `README.md` ("What it will never do"), `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `docs/architecture.md`, `docs/agent-workflow.md` §9, `docs/ui-brief.md`, `docs/design/README.md` (the copy table's bolus row: "the only insulin concept: a timestamp"), `docs/architecture.md` also gains the data model and events of §3–4, `shared/src/glucose/import/types.ts` (the Clarity comment "not even as a stored number": still true for the import, reworded so it does not state the old rule), `docs/plugins/glucose-source.md` and `docs/plugins/food-source.md` (their rule lines), `.github/pull_request_template.md`.

## 8. What is left out, and why (decision 4)

Temp Basal arrives about 400 times a day, is not a bolus, and says nothing about a meal's timing; showing basal rates is a different product (a pump viewer) and a much larger change to the rule. Sleep is a pump mode, not a treatment Frejsi has a use for. Both are filtered out in the query, so they never reach Frejsi. Extended boluses are stored with the units Nightscout has for them and drawn at their start; how tconnectsync splits the immediate and extended part is not modelled.

## 9. Design gaps and how the lanes fill them

No artboard shows pump markers, the pump-meal badge or the Settings card. The lanes build them from existing tokens and components and record them in `docs/design/`:

1. **Automatic bolus marker** (task 4): a 7 px hollow triangle in the text colour at 60 % on the baseline, `chart.autoBolusMarker` in `tokens.json`. Terracotta stays the meal's; an automatic bolus is information, not a meal.
2. **Units on the curve** (task 4): desktop day curves label a manual triangle with its units in 11 px text above it ("2,4"); the phone shows no labels and opens a small popover on tap with time, units and the kind; the meal curve labels its own bolus on both.

## 13. Decisions taken by the planner

1. **One Nightscout site, two connections.** The pump record has its own config, status and secret (`pump-<spaceId>`) beside the glucose source, so a Dexcom Share space can read its pump from Nightscout and a Nightscout space can use the same site twice without the two sharing a failure count.
2. **The connect path is `source-connect` with a `purpose`.** A second function would need a second URL and key in the web app's settings (an infra change); the existing function already probes Nightscout and writes Key Vault. The strict pump schema is tried first so a pump connect can never write the glucose secret.
3. **A 15-minute timer of its own.** The data lags up to an hour, so every five minutes is waste; a separate function keeps a slow Nightscout from delaying the glucose poll.
4. **Boluses only** (§8). Basal is the larger, different change to the rule; the owner's ask is meal timing and the bolus record.
5. **Units on `PumpBolus`, not on `Meal`.** The meal links to the bolus; units are read through the link, so there is one record of what the pump delivered, and an unlinked meal carries nothing.
6. **Pump carbs: match, show, and create an unverified meal after two hours** (§6). Matching and "Pumpen: 45 g" keep the logged meal the person's; the pump meal gives the statistics a meal when nobody logged one, like the Clarity import; the two-hour grace and the removal of an untouched pump meal stop double meals when someone logs late; a dismissed pump meal stays gone. The alternative, never creating meals from pump carbs, is simpler and loses the statistics for every meal entered only in the pump.
7. **Window 45 minutes before to 30 after.** A pre-bolus is rarely longer than 30–40 minutes; a bolus after the first bites is common with children. Nearest wins, so a snack's bolus does not steal lunch's.
8. **Automatic boluses never attach and never make meals.** Control-IQ's own boluses are about a third of all and have no carbs; attaching one to a meal would make its lead minutes fiction.
9. **Pump meals are hidden from viewers until ticked.** The owner's rule says no pump data to a viewer; an untouched pump meal is pump data. A ticked one is the household's meal and a viewer sees it with its carbs, like any meal (§14 asks the owner to confirm).
10. **Pump-caused meal changes travel in the glucose group.** A `meal.updated` from the cycle would tell a viewer when pump data arrives; `pump.meals` carries the ids to owners and carers only.
11. **No new artboard.** The pieces are markers, a badge and a card built from existing tokens; §9 records them in `docs/design/`, and the owner can send them through Claude Design later.
12. **Gates on for this milestone.** The Claude gates are off until v1.0.0, but this milestone rewrites a hard rule; `spec-guardian`, `reviewer` and `copy-reviewer` run on every lane (`gates: true`). Task 1 runs on Fable.
