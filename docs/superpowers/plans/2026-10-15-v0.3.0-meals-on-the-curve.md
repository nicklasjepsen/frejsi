## 1. What v0.3.0 is

When `v0.3.0` is tagged and `release.yml` is green, the owner opens a meal and sees the following hours of glucose under it (the success criterion of spec §0):

- **Meal detail** (`Måltid`): the glucose curve from one hour before to four hours after `meal.ts`, the meal line and the bolus triangle, the same dish's previous curves faintly behind (three on the phone, five on desktop), the three `StatTile`s (peak and time to peak, time in range over 3 h, the gap between the bolus tap and the meal), the items with their carbs, and the photo. On desktop also "Sammen med de sidste 5 gange" with the compare table, previous/next meal with `←`/`→`, "Ret" with `E`, and "Gem som egen ret". In simpleMode a calm "Du spiste kl. 12:10" view with the photo, the items and the total, nothing else.
- **Edit and delete from meal detail**: "Ret" opens the confirm screen the family knows from logging (time, portions, the bolus tap, note, photo) inside meal detail; "Slet måltid" deletes after a question. Both go through v0.2.0's `PATCH`/`DELETE meals/[mealId]` and therefore `meal.updated`/`meal.deleted` through `emit()`. The `log?meal=` workaround and its entry points are retired.
- **Statistics in code** (§5.4): `shared/src/stats/` computes peak, time to peak, time in range 4–10 over the next 3 h, the bolus lead in minutes, the "same dish" key the server uses to find previous occurrences, the day's and the week's numbers. Pure functions on readings, tested on fixtures, computed on read, run on the server for responses and on the client for live updates. No model is involved anywhere.
- **History** (`Historik`): on the phone, days as a scrollable timeline of meals over the day's curve (today first, the drawn previous/next-day arrows, older days as you scroll), each meal row with its peak; the week behind the "Ugen" header link. On desktop the week with seven day tiles and "Ugens tal", the day with its curve and timeline, and "Måltider" as a table of the week's meals, with `←`/`→` between weeks and days. A viewer gets "Tidligere dage": meals and carb totals per day from `GET day`, no curve and nothing derived from glucose, enforced in the API.
- **Pre-bolus timing made visible, as information**: "Bolus 11:58" on the curve, the tile "Bolus · 12 min · før måltidet", the "Bolus før" column, the week's "Bolus før måltid · 9 min · i gennemsnit, 17 af 23 registreret". Never an amount, never a unit, never a sentence that says what to do.
- **The readings query for a window around a meal**, `GET meals/[mealId]/curve`, plus `GET history/day` and `GET history/week`: owner and carer (the subject is one of them, §2) get readings; a viewer gets 403, enforced in the route, with a test.
- **Live** through `useLiveFeed` only: a meal eaten an hour ago grows its curve from the hook's `readings` as they arrive; the history's today does the same; a `meal.*` event through the hook's `subscribe` refetches. Screens never fetch live data themselves; they fetch history once and read the hook. `web/src/lib/live/` is not changed.
- **Release**: Playwright on the meal detail flow (phone and desktop) and the viewer's history, Prisma integration tests for the window query and the dish matching, the seed extended with two weeks of readings and earlier occurrences of a dish so the compare view has data. `release.yml` is not changed.

## 4. Zod contracts (`shared/src/schemas/history.ts`)

All `.strict()`. Every view below carries glucose and is therefore **owner/carer only**; a test asserts that `DayView` (v0.2.0, the viewer's) and the seven v0.2.0 event payloads keep their key sets (snapshot), and that `DayView`'s key set walked recursively contains none of §9's viewer forbidden keys.

- `Target = { low: number, high: number }` in mmol (from `Space.targetLow/High`, v0.1.0 §3); the client converts for mg/dL spaces through `shared/src/glucose/units.ts`.
- `InRange = { minutes: number, coveredMinutes: number, windowMinutes: number, complete: boolean, belowMinutes: number, aboveMinutes: number }`: `minutes` within `[low, high]` inclusive, `coveredMinutes` the minutes a reading accounts for (§5), `windowMinutes` the full window (180 for a meal), `complete` false while `now < window.to`.
- `MealStats = { peak?: { mmol: number, ts: ISO datetime, minutesAfterMeal: number }, inRange: InRange, bolusLeadMinutes?: number, readingCount: number }`. `peak` is absent when the stats window has no reading. `bolusLeadMinutes` is `round((meal.ts − bolusTs) / 60 s)`, positive when the tap came before the meal, absent when the meal has no `bolusTs`. There is no other field about the bolus: not an amount, not a unit, not a ratio, not a suggestion (type test; the dosing test runs on the schema keys as in v0.1.0 §8; `bolusLeadMinutes` is one camelCase key like v0.2.0's `bolusTs`).
- `PreviousMealView = { mealId, ts, title, totalCarbs, bolusLeadMinutes?, stats: MealStats, readings: Reading[] }` — the same dish's earlier occurrence with its own −1 h/+4 h readings (absolute `ts`; the chart positions them relative to that meal's `ts`).
- `Neighbours = { previousMealId?: string, nextMealId?: string }` — the meals before and after by `ts` in the space, for `←`/`→`.
- `EditItem = { foodId?: string, name, portionLabel?, grams?, carbs, portions?: Portion[], carbsPer100g?: number }` — what the edit sheet needs that v0.2.0's `MealItemView` deliberately lacks (`foodId`, the food's current portions and per-100 g value); an item whose food was deleted has no `foodId` and edits as typed carbs. Owner/carer only, like everything in this file.
- `MealCurveView = { meal: MealView, editItems: EditItem[], window: { from, to }, target: Target, readings: Reading[], stats: MealStats, previous: PreviousMealView[] (0–5, newest first), neighbours: Neighbours, now: ISO datetime }`.
- `DayHistoryMeal = DayMealView & { bolusTs?, stats: MealStats }`; `DayHistoryView = { date, timezone, from, to, target, readings: Reading[] (empty with `?readings=0`), inRange: InRange, totalCarbs, mealCount, meals: DayHistoryMeal[] (newest first), hasOlder: boolean (a meal or a reading exists before `from`), now }`.
- `WeekDayTile = { date, mealCount, totalCarbs, inRangePercent?: number, curve: { ts, mmol }[] (≤ 48 points), mealTimes: ISO datetime[] }`; `WeekNumbers = { inRangePercent?, previousWeekInRangePercent?, belowPercent?, lowEpisodes: { count, atNight }, carbsPerDay, mealCount, bolusLead: { meanMinutes?, registered, of } }`; `MealStatsRow = { mealId, ts, title, totalCarbs, bolusLeadMinutes?, stats: MealStats }`; `WeekHistoryView = { weekStart, weekEnd, isoWeek, isoYear, timezone, target, days: WeekDayTile[7], numbers: WeekNumbers, meals: MealStatsRow[] (newest first), now }`.
- No new event types and no new `ApiError` codes: v0.3.0 reuses `meal.updated`, `meal.deleted`, `food.created`, `food.updated` and the codes `forbidden`, `not-found`, `validation`, `simple-mode`.

## 5. The statistics (spec §5.4), deterministic and in code

All in `shared/src/stats/`, pure, timezone passed in, no `Date.now()` inside (the caller passes `now`). Readings are in mmol (v0.1.0 decision 13); conversion is rendering only.

- **Windows.** Chart window `[meal.ts − 60 min, meal.ts + 240 min]`; stats window `[meal.ts, meal.ts + 180 min]` (§5.4 "over the next 3 h"); the chart shows a fourth hour for context, the numbers stop at three so "I mål 3 t af 3 t" is exact. A window that extends past `now` is `complete: false` and the copy says "indtil nu".
- **Peak and time to peak.** The highest `mmol` among readings with `ts` in the stats window, ties to the earliest; `minutesAfterMeal = round((peak.ts − meal.ts) / 60 s)`. Shown as "Top 9,6 · efter 1 t 05 min".
- **Time in range, time-weighted and gap-capped.** A reading at `ts` with successor `next` covers `[ts, min(next.ts, window.to, now))` when `next.ts − ts ≤ COVERAGE_CAP_MINUTES` (15), and nothing otherwise; a reading with no successor covers nothing; readings before `window.from` are clipped to it. The minutes before the first reading in the window and any gap beyond the cap are uncovered (neither in nor out of range). `minutes` counts covered time with `low ≤ mmol ≤ high`; `belowMinutes` and `aboveMinutes` the rest. Readings every 5 minutes with no gap give `coveredMinutes = 180` for a complete meal window; a 30-minute gap takes 30 covered minutes away (the reading before the gap covers nothing). The day's "I mål 4–10: 8 t 50 min af 9 t 13 min" is `minutes af coveredMinutes`; the week's "I mål 81 %" is `minutes / coveredMinutes` over the week, rounded to a whole percent, absent when nothing is covered.
- **Bolus lead.** `bolusLeadMinutes = round((meal.ts − meal.bolusTs) / 60 s)`; positive before the meal ("12 min · før måltidet"), zero ("0 min"), negative after ("5 min · efter måltidet"), absent without a tap ("– · ikke registreret"). The week's mean is over meals with a tap, shown with "17 af 23 registreret". It is a difference of two timestamps the carer entered; nothing in `stats/` reads or computes anything else about insulin: a word-bounded grep test over the folder finds none of `unit`, `units`, `ratio`, `dose`, `correction`, and `stats/` never imports `shared/src/glucose/units.ts` (conversion is rendering only), so the grep is unambiguous.
- **Same dish.** `dishKey(items)` = the sorted, de-duplicated set of item keys, where an item's key is `food:<foodId>` when `foodId` is set and `name:<foldForSearch(name)>` otherwise (v0.2.0 `shared/src/food/search.ts`), joined by `\n`. Two meals are the same dish when their keys are equal: same foods regardless of portions (the artboard's table varies 36–50 g), and a food deleted later (`foodId` nulled, name kept) still matches by name. Previous occurrences are the newest five (three shown on the phone) with `ts < meal.ts`; the server finds them (§6), `shared/` only defines the key.
- **Low episodes** (the week's "Under 4 · 3 gange, alle om natten"): a run of readings below `low` starts an episode; a new episode needs at least 30 minutes at or above `low` in between; `atNight` counts episodes whose first reading falls between 22:00 and 06:00 in the space's timezone.
- **Day tiles.** `downsample(readings, 30)` averages readings per 30-minute bucket from the day's start (≤ 48 points) for the 120×64 week tile; meal dots are the meals' `ts`.
- **Week.** ISO week (Monday–Sunday) in the space's timezone, through v0.2.0's `dayRange` for each day; "Uge 42 · 6.–12. oktober"; `previousWeekInRangePercent` from the `previousWeekReadings` argument; `carbsPerDay = round(sum / 7)`.
- **Windows for days and weeks extend past their end by the stats window**: the day service loads readings in `[day.from, day.to + 180 min]` and the week service `[week.from, week.to + 180 min]` (plus the previous week), so a 21:30 meal has the same `stats` on `history/day`, `history/week` and `curve`; the day's own `inRange` and the week's percent use `[from, to]` only.
- **Formatting is the client's.** `stats/` returns minutes, percents and timestamps; `web/messages/*.json` holds "{h} t {m} min", "efter {duration}", "af {duration}", "indtil nu", "før måltidet", "efter måltidet" (the hours word "t" is a `da` string, "h" an `en` string; nothing Danish in `shared/`). Decimal comma in `da`, point in `en`, whole numbers for mg/dL spaces (v0.1.0 `GlucoseValue` rules).

## 6. API routes and services

All under `web/src/app/api/spaces/[spaceId]/`; every handler starts with `requireRole(spaceId, "owner", "carer")` (v0.1.0 §6), so a `viewer` is 403 before any query and a non-member 404. The services in `web/src/lib/history/` read through `HistoryRepository` (`readingsBetween(spaceId, from, to)`, `mealsBetween(spaceId, from, to)` with items and the current `Food` rows, `mealById`, `candidateMeals(spaceId, before, foodIds, limit)`, `recentMeals(spaceId, before, limit)`, `neighbours(spaceId, ts)`, `hasAnythingBefore(spaceId, ts)`, `space(spaceId)` for target and timezone), implemented by `PrismaHistoryRepository` and faked by `MemoryHistoryRepository`, as `FoodRepository` is in v0.2.0; they run `shared/src/stats/`, resolve photo thumbnails through `PhotoStore.readUrl` (v0.2.0 §6), and **write nothing**. Log lines carry `spaceId`, `userId`, `route`, `count`, `code` and `ms` only (v0.1.0's `LOG_KEYS` in `shared/src/log.ts`, which no v0.3.0 task widens; a meal id is not logged): never a reading value, a title or a note.

| Route | Roles | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| `GET meals/[mealId]/curve` | owner, carer | `MealCurveView`: the meal as `MealView` (v0.2.0) plus `editItems` (each item joined with its current `Food` for `foodId`, `portions`, `carbsPer100g`), readings in the chart window (one range query on the clustered PK), `stats` from the stats window, up to five previous occurrences of the same dish each with its readings and stats (candidates: `candidateMeals` by the meal's `foodId`s when every item has one, otherwise the 200 newest earlier meals through `recentMeals`; confirmed by `dishKey` equality in code; newest first), `neighbours` by `ts`, and `now`. 404 for a meal of another space (not 403, so ids do not leak). |
| `GET history/day?date=YYYY-MM-DD&readings=0\|1` | owner, carer | `DayHistoryView` for the space's local day (`dayRange` with `Space.timezone`), default today: readings in `[from, min(to, now)]` (omitted with `readings=0`, for the Now screen that already has v0.1.0's `readings?hours=24`), `inRange` over the covered part of the day, the meals newest first with `bolusTs` and per-meal `stats` computed over `[from, to + 180 min]`, and `hasOlder`. Invalid date: 400 `validation`. |
| `GET history/week?start=YYYY-MM-DD` | owner, carer | `WeekHistoryView` for the ISO week starting on that Monday (default: the current week; a non-Monday is 400): seven `WeekDayTile`s, `numbers` (including the previous week's in-range percent), and the week's meals as `MealStatsRow`s with stats over `[from, to + 180 min]`. Two reading queries (this week plus three hours, the previous week), ≈ 4 000 rows at most. |
| `GET day` (v0.2.0, unchanged) | owner, carer, viewer | the only meal read a viewer may call; still carries no `bolusTs`, note or glucose. The viewer's "Tidligere dage" is built on it alone. |

Writes in v0.3.0 go only through v0.2.0's `PATCH meals/[mealId]` (`updateMeal` → `meal.updated`), `DELETE meals/[mealId]` (`deleteMeal` → `meal.deleted`), `PUT meals/[mealId]/photo`, `POST foods` and `PUT foods/[foodId]/photo` (`food.created`, `food.updated`). The screens call these through `fetch`; no v0.3.0 path imports Prisma for a write (lint boundary from v0.2.0).

## 7. Charts: hand-written SVG, deliberately

The curves are **hand-written SVG components** in `web/src/components/glucose/`, not a charting library:

- The artboards are literal SVG with fixed viewBoxes (`tokens.json#chart`: `curvePhone` 326×118, `curveDesktopMeal` 760×262, `curveDesktopDay` 720×220, `weekTile` 120×64), stroke widths (2.5 main, 1.5 at 0.18/0.16 previous, 1.8 week), the range band at `accent.band` 0.12, dashed bounds (3 4), the meal line in `meal.marker`, the bolus triangle (10 px, `meal.bolusMarker`, on the baseline at the bolus time), the end dot r 5 and the axis labels at 11/12 px; a library would be configured to reproduce exactly this and would fight the tokens on every detail.
- v0.1.0 already draws the sparkline and the day curve by hand in the same folder (`GlucoseChart` variants `sparkline-3h` and `day`); v0.3.0 adds the `meal` and `week-tile` variants and meal markers on `day`. One component model, one set of scale helpers.
- The phone PWA stays small: no 100 KB+ dependency for five polylines. There is no interaction beyond tapping a meal line (phone: opens the meal) and keyboard focus on desktop; no zoom, no tooltips.

## 12. Decisions taken by the planner

Each is a choice the spec or the design left open, with the reason. The owner can veto any of them by opening a `needs-owner` issue.

1. **Statistics are computed on read, not stored.** Readings are immutable but arrive for hours after a meal (a curve eaten an hour ago is still growing), so stored numbers would be stale or need recomputation on every reading; the volumes are tiny (≤ 61 readings per meal window, ≤ 2 016 per week); and the same pure functions run on the client for live updates. No schema change in v0.3.0.
2. **"Same dish" is the set of foods regardless of portions**, keyed by `foodId` or, for items without one, the folded name. The artboard's compare table varies 36–50 g across occurrences, so portions must not matter; the name fallback keeps a deleted food's history. No stored key: candidates come from `MealItem.foodId`/`name` and are confirmed in code.
3. **The stats window is 3 h, the chart window −1 h/+4 h.** The spec says "over the next 3 h"; the artboards draw four hours and say "I mål af 3 t". The chart shows the fourth hour for context; the numbers stop at three so the tile is exact. The phone tile's drawn footnote "af 3 t indtil nu" is read as the incomplete state: the plan shows "indtil nu" only while the window is still open and "af 3 t" once it is complete, as the desktop tile draws.
4. **Time in range is time-weighted with a 15-minute coverage cap.** A reading accounts for the time until the next, at most 15 minutes; a sensor-change gap is neither in nor out of range. Counting readings would make a gap look like time in range.
5. **Hand-written SVG, no chart library** (§7): the artboards are literal SVG with tokens for every stroke; v0.1.0 already draws by hand; the PWA stays small; the components are testable in jsdom.
6. **Edit lives in meal detail and reuses the confirm screen's parts.** The artboard's "Ret" points at `Log-Portioner`; v0.3.0 mounts those parts as a sheet owned by meal detail and retires `log?meal=` (v0.2.0 decision 29 said meal detail would take the entry points over). Delete sits in the same sheet. The desktop side panel for the sheet is undrawn (design gap) and is derived from `Log-Desktop`'s meal column.
7. **The history day curve spans the whole local day (00:00–24:00)**, not 06:00 to now as `Historik-Telefon` draws. The week's numbers call out night lows ("3 gange, alle om natten"), and a parent reviewing a day on a Sunday evening needs the night; Now keeps its 06:00 start (today so far). The axis labels become 00 · 06 · 12 · 18 · nu/24.
8. **The phone week view is derived** from the desktop week (tiles stacked, "Ugens tal" 2-up, "Forrige uge" / "Næste uge") behind the drawn "Ugen" header link, with a "Dag" link back to the timeline; no artboard exists (design gap). The phone "Ugens opsummering" card is omitted until v0.4.0 because its content is the AI summary.
9. **The simpleMode meal detail shows no curve and no numbers**: `Nu-Simpel` shows no chart, and `Maaltid-Simpel` only draws the AI estimate, so the child's view is the photo, the items and the total. A carer sees the curve on their own phone.
10. **The viewer's "Tidligere dage" is built on `GET day` alone**, one day per section, no new viewer route, so the API-level guarantee of v0.2.0 (no glucose key in `DayView`) covers history too.
11. **No new routes for the week numbers on the client**: `history/week` returns the tiles downsampled to 30-minute buckets (≤ 48 points) and the numbers, so a week costs one request and ≈ 20 KB instead of 2 016 readings.
12. **`GET meals/[mealId]/curve` is the readings-around-a-meal query** rather than a generic `readings?from&to`: one call gives the phone everything the screen shows, and the role gate sits on one route. v0.1.0's `readings?hours=` is untouched.
13. **Pre-bolus timing is a number with a position word**, never a sentence: "Bolus · 12 min · før måltidet", "Bolus før" in tables, "9 min · i gennemsnit, 17 af 23 registreret" in the week. The allow-list grows by seven enumerated strings (§9) in task 3 only; the copy test of §9 refuses advice verbs under the stats, chart, meal and history keys. The narration that relates timing to outcome is v0.4.0 and remains observation there.
14. **"Gem som egen ret" is client-composed** from v0.2.0's `POST foods` and the photo route: one family food with a single "1 portion" of the meal's total carbs, `verified: false` like every created food; no new server code.
15. **"Gem som PDF til klinikken" is omitted**: data export is unscheduled in the roadmap; "Spørgsmål til klinikken", the `Observation` blocks and "Ugens opsummering" are omitted as v0.4.0, not drawn disabled, as v0.1.0 and v0.2.0 did with theirs.
16. **The `Nu-Desktop` meal markers and "Top" column land in task 8** with the other entry points, because they need task 2's `history/day` and task 3's chart variant; v0.2.0 left them out on purpose.
17. **The `ChartCaption` "sidste måling for {age}"** is plan-added copy under any curve whose window includes now, so the live end of a curve is never a value without its age (the artboards draw the end dot alone).
18. **Low episodes** use a 30-minute recovery rule and "night" is 22:00–06:00 local: the artboard's "3 gange, alle om natten" needs a definition; these are conventional and stated in `stats/`.
