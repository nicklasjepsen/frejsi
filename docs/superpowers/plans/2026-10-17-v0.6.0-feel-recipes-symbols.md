## 1. What v0.6.0 is

When `v0.6.0` is tagged and `release.yml` is green:

- **"Jeg har det…"** (#194). A symptom is its own entry next to meals: kind (six defaults, `da`/`en`), time, who logged it, optional severity and note, optional resolved time. Two taps on the phone: the button on Now opens a picture-first picker, a tap logs the kind at now. Severity, note and another time are a second, optional step. A carer can edit or delete any symptom in the space; a viewer sees them as they see meals; a simpleMode member logs but does not edit afterwards, sees no severity scale, and a symptom they log pushes to the carers like "Spørg en voksen" does. Symptoms are events on the live feed, markers on the live curve and in day and week history (their own shape and colour, never mistaken for a meal), a filter in history, and lines in the Say sheet ("jeg har ondt i maven" becomes a symptom, not a meal). The AI observations may mention them next to meals, never explain them.
- **A dish from a recipe** (#195). "Ny ret fra opskrift" takes a photo, a URL or pasted text (and a shared page, text or image from the phone's share sheet, or the items of a logged meal), reads the ingredient lines and the yield (a `schema.org/Recipe` page needs no model; text and photos use the provider, so those two ways are absent when AI is off), resolves every line through `lookup()` in the normal order with no-carb staples at 0, and shows the lines, the matches, the carbs per line, the total and a yield field. Nothing is saved before the tap. Saving creates an own dish of origin `recipe`, `verified: false`, with carbs per 100 g and the named portions derived from the ingredients and the yield, the source kept, the photo as the dish photo. Editing an ingredient or the yield recalculates the dish; past meals keep their snapshots.
- **What was eaten, on the curve** (#196). Every food carries a category from a fixed set of ten; Frida's groups and Open Food Facts' category tags map to it at import, the enrich-foods timer fills the rest, a carer sets it with one chip row in the food form. A meal's symbol is derived when it is saved (the dominant item's category, the plate for a mixed meal, the apple when nothing is known) and stored on the meal, so a library edit never rewrites a past day. The symbol is drawn at the top of each meal marker on desktop Now, in history day and week and on the meal curve, in the meal colour, `aria-hidden`, with the category in the marker's accessible name; the legend's meal entry shows the plate.
- **Editing a logged meal, one tap from wherever it is shown** (the owner, 2026-10-17: "I also want to be able to edit a meal after it's been logged"). The edit sheet of v0.3.0 (items, portions, time, note, photo, bolus time) exists behind "Ret" on meal detail, and on the phone that is a text link reached only after opening the meal. In v0.6.0 every meal row on phone Now and in history day opens the meal, each row carries a 44 px "Ret" that opens the meal with the sheet already open, and meal detail's own "Ret" is a proper button on the phone. Nothing changes in what can be edited or who may edit (owner and carer; a simpleMode member logs and does not edit afterwards, as #194 decides for symptoms).
- **Release**: seed data with symptoms, categories and one recipe dish; Playwright for the four flows on phone and desktop; the runbook and README.

## 3. Prisma schema (task 1, migration `0005_feel`)

- `Symptom`: `id` cuid, `spaceId`, `ts DateTime`, `byUserId`, `kind NVarChar(40)`, `severity NVarChar(10)?`, `note NVarChar(500)?`, `resolvedTs DateTime?`, `createdAt`, `updatedAt @updatedAt`; relations to `Space` and `User` with `NoAction` like `Meal`; `@@index([spaceId, ts])`. Back-relations `Space.symptoms`, `User.symptoms`.
- `Space.symptomKinds NVarChar(Max) @default("[]")`: JSON `SpaceSymptomKind[]` (`{ id: "custom:<slug>", label: { da, en } }`), validated by Zod, no editor in v0.6.0.
- `Food.category NVarChar(20)?` (a `FoodCategory` or null = unknown), `Food.recipe NVarChar(Max)?` (JSON `Recipe`).
- `Meal.symbol NVarChar(20)?` (a `MealSymbol`; null on rows from before 0005, rendered as the apple).
- The snapshot `theme.prisma` freezes the schema as of 0004; `prisma:check` diffs `theme.prisma → schema.prisma` against `0005_feel/migration.sql`.

## 4. Zod contracts (task 1)

- `FoodCategory = enum(bread, fruit, vegetables, dairy, starch, protein, sweets, snack, drink, mixed)`; `MealSymbol = FoodCategory | "unknown"`. `starch` is pasta, rice and potato; `protein` is meat, fish and egg; `mixed` is the plate.
- `mealSymbol(items: { carbs, category? }[]): MealSymbol` (in `shared/src/meals/symbol.ts`): no items → `unknown`; the item with the most carbs is dominant; if the total is 0 the first item is dominant; dominant without a category → `unknown`; dominant carbs ≥ half the total → its category; otherwise `mixed`.
- `SymptomKind`: the six defaults `stomach-ache`, `dizzy`, `nausea`, `tired`, `headache`, `shaky`, or `custom:<slug>` present in the space's `symptomKinds`. `Severity = light | medium | strong`. `CreateSymptomRequest { kind, ts? (default now, not more than 10 min ahead, not older than 7 days), severity?, note? ≤ 500 }`, `UpdateSymptomRequest` partial with `resolvedTs` nullable, `SymptomView { id, kind, ts, severity?, note?, resolvedTs?, byUserId, byName }`, `SymptomsView { symptoms }`, `SymptomRangeQuery { from, to }` (≤ 8 days).
- `Recipe { ingredients: RecipeIngredient[] (1–40), yieldGrams?, yieldPortions?, source: RecipeSource }` with `RecipeIngredient { name ≤ 120, amount?, unit? ≤ 20, grams?, foodId?, foodName?, carbs int, noCarb? }` and `RecipeSource { kind: photo | url | text | meal, url?, text? ≤ 4000, photoKey?, mealId? }`; `hasYield` refine: one of `yieldGrams`, `yieldPortions`. `RecipeParse` (AI output): `{ title ≤ 120, ingredients: { name, amount?, unit? }[] ≤ 40, yieldPortions?, yieldGrams? }`. `RecipeParseRequest = { text } | { url } | { photoDataUrl }` (the estimate's data-URL bound). `RecipeReview { title, lines: (RecipeIngredient & { match?: FoodView, candidates?: FoodCandidate[] })[], totalCarbs, yieldPortions?, yieldGrams?, source, approx: boolean }`. `AiUse` gains `"recipe"`.
- `FoodCandidate.category?`, `Food.category?`, `Food.recipe?`, `FoodView.category?`, `FoodView.recipe?`, `CreateFoodRequest.category?`, `.recipe?`, `UpdateFoodRequest` likewise; `MealView.symbol`, `DayMealView.symbol`, `DayHistoryMeal.symbol`; `WeekDayTile.meals: { ts, symbol }[]` (the existing `mealTimes` stays, append-only).
- `ParsedMeal.symptoms?: { kind: SymptomKind, note? }[]` (default `[]`). `InsightInput.symptoms?: { kind, minutesAfterMeal }[]`; `WeekInsightInput.symptomCounts?: { kind, count }[]`. Kinds and counts only: never a reading, never a note.
- Events, appended: `symptom.created { symptomId, ts, kind, byUserId }`, `symptom.updated { symptomId }`, `symptom.deleted { symptomId }`. Not glucose events: viewers receive them.

## 5. Symptoms end to end (#194, task 3 and task 5)

Routes under `/api/spaces/[spaceId]/symptoms`: `GET ?from&to` for every role (viewers included: a symptom carries no glucose), `POST` for owner and carer (a simpleMode session may `POST` with the default severity only), `PATCH` and `DELETE` for owner and carer not in simpleMode. Every write goes through one transaction with `emit()` and publishes after commit, like meals. `WebPushSink` gains a `symptom.created` branch: when the logger is a simpleMode member, every other owner or carer with a live subscription gets "{child}: {kind}, {time}" and a deep link to `/{locale}/history?day=…`; nothing when the logger is not in simpleMode.

The picker (`/feel`): six large tiles with a picture and a name (the symptoms namespace in `da`/`en`), plus the space's custom kinds; one tap logs at now and shows a confirmation with "Fortryd" for 10 seconds; "Mere…" opens the sheet with severity chips, a note and a time field. In simpleMode the page is the picker only, 84 px tiles, no sheet. The desktop sidebar gets "Jeg har det…" (task 3); Now gets the phone button under "Log et måltid" and the simple Now one under "Jeg spiser nu" (task 5 mounts task 3's `FeelButton`).

Say: `ParsedMeal.symptoms` is filled by the parser when the text names a feeling; the sheet shows symptom lines apart from food lines, and saving creates the symptoms through the symptoms route and the meal through the meals route only when there are food items ("jeg har ondt i maven" alone logs a symptom and no meal).

AI observations: the meal narration input lists the symptoms within −1 h/+4 h of the meal as kind and minutes; the week input lists counts per kind. The prompts say to mention them as logged, next to the meal, and never to explain or diagnose; `guardOutput` and the dosing test apply as before.

The curve (task 5): a symptom marker is a small diamond on the time axis in the glucose text colour (`--text`), never terracotta, with a 44 px tap target named "{kind} kl. {time}", on desktop Now, history day, the meal curve (within the window) and the week tile (a dot below the meal dots). Tapping shows kind, severity, note, who logged it and the readings around it (the existing day data; a viewer's tap shows no readings). History gets a "Symptomer" chip beside the period header that filters the day list to symptoms.

## 6. A dish from a recipe (#195, task 4)

`parseRecipe` (`shared/src/ai/recipe/`): a URL is fetched by `fetchRecipePage` (https only, 2 MB and 10 s caps, no redirects off the origin, user agent "Frejsi"), and when the page has `application/ld+json` with `@type: Recipe`, `readJsonLd` returns the `RecipeParse` without a model call (`recipeIngredient[]` parsed by the amount-unit-name grammar, `recipeYield`); otherwise the page text (tags stripped, ≤ 12 000 chars) goes to `provider.complete({ role: "everyday", use: "recipe", outputSchema: RecipeParse })`; pasted text goes the same way; a photo goes to `provider.vision` as the estimate does. `resolveIngredients` runs `lookup()` per line in the normal order with `remote: true`, takes the first library match, else the first candidate, else no match; a line whose name is in `NO_CARB_STAPLES` (`da`/`en`: water, salt, pepper, spices, oil, butter, vinegar, …) resolves to 0 without a lookup; grams per line come from amount and unit (g, kg, dl, l, spsk, tsk, stk with a per-food default where the match has a portion). `computeRecipe` gives carbs per 100 g from the total and `yieldGrams` (or the sum of ingredient grams when only portions are given), and the portions "1 portion", "½ portion" from `yieldPortions`.

The review form shows every line (name, amount, matched food, carbs, "approx." when the line came from a model and no human has changed it), lets the person change the match (search), the amount or remove the line, and set the yield and the portion names; the total and carbs per 100 g update live. Save → `POST recipes` → `createFood({ name, origin: "recipe", carbsPer100g, portions, category?, recipe, verify: false })` then `setFoodPhoto` from the recipe photo when there is one; `PATCH recipes/[foodId]` recalculates from edited lines. Ways in: the library button and the log's manual path open `/recipe/new`; "Gem som opskrift" on meal detail opens it with the meal's items as lines and the eaten amounts as the yield of one portion (`source.kind = "meal"`); the share sheet `POST`s to `/api/share` (`share_target` in the manifest, `multipart/form-data` with `title`, `text`, `url`, `files[]`), which stores the share under a random id for ten minutes (memory in the web process, one per session) and redirects to `/{locale}/recipe/new?share=<id>`; the form's microphone (the Say sheet's `MicButton`) dictates into the text field.

Off when AI is off: the photo and free-text ways are hidden and the route answers 409 `ai-disabled` for them; the URL way still works when the page has JSON-LD and otherwise answers `recipe-unreadable`. The photo and the fetched page reach the provider only from the explicit tap on the form; the URL is fetched only by that request.

## 7. Food categories and the symbol (#196, tasks 2 and 5)

Frida: the 118 groups in `frida.json` map to the ten categories by a table in `shared/src/food/category/frida-groups.ts` (every group present, asserted by a test). Open Food Facts: `fields()` adds `categories_tags`, `map.ts` picks the most specific known tag by a table of about forty `en:` tags (`en:breads` → bread, `en:fruits` → fruit, `en:dairies` → dairy, `en:pastas`, `en:rices`, `en:potatoes` → starch, `en:meats`, `en:fishes`, `en:eggs` → protein, `en:sweets`, `en:cakes`, `en:chocolates` → sweets, `en:snacks` → snack, `en:beverages` → drink, …); unknown → no category. `addCandidate` recomputes the category server-side for Frida picks. The enrich-foods timer gets a third pass: up to 50 foods per run with `category: null` and an origin other than `recipe`, `categoriseFood` (role small, the normalise cap) writes the category; the row stays `verified: false` as it was. The food form gets a chip row "Kategori" with the ten symbols and names; the library rows show the symbol before the name.

The symbol: `mealSymbol()` runs in `createMeal` and whenever `updateMeal` changes the items; the value is stored on `Meal.symbol` and never recomputed from the library afterwards. Every meal view carries it (task 1 plumbing). `FoodSymbol` draws the ten symbols and the apple as inline SVG, stroke `currentColor`, round caps, on a 24-unit viewBox, legible at 12 px: bread (a loaf), fruit (an apple), vegetables (a carrot), dairy (a glass), starch (a bowl with a fork), protein (a drumstick), sweets (a lollipop), snack (a pretzel), drink (a cup with a straw), mixed (a plate), unknown (the apple again). The chart draws it centred on the meal line at `markerTop` (day variant, 12 px on the phone's sizes, 16 px on desktop) and above `plotTop` (meal variant), in `--meal-marker`, `aria-hidden`; the tap button's name becomes "Måltid, {category}, kl. {time}" and "Måltid kl. {time}" when the symbol is unknown; the legend's meal entry draws the plate. The bolus triangle is untouched.

## 8. Design gaps and how the lanes fill them

The three features have no artboards. Rather than wait for a Claude Design round, each lane builds from the existing components and tokens, and the owner can redraw afterwards (task 7 lists what to look at):

- **The symbol set** (#196 asks for the drawings in `docs/design/` before the lane starts): task 5 draws them in code under the `icons` rules of `tokens.json` and writes `docs/design/food-symbols.md` in the same PR with the eleven SVGs, their names in `da`/`en` and the sizes. A later Claude Design pass replaces the paths, nothing else.
- **The symptom picker** is the simpleMode food picker's component family (`SimplePicker` tiles, `ChipGroup`, `BackButton`) with six pictures drawn like the symbols.
- **The recipe review form** is the meal edit list (`EditMealItems` rows) plus the food form's portions fieldset and `PhotoPicker`.
- **The symptom marker** is a diamond in the text colour; the design rule "meals are always terracotta" is kept by giving nothing else that colour.

## 10. CI and lane discipline

Shared append-only files that no task owns and any task may append to (`docs/agent-workflow.md` §3): `web/messages/da/index.ts` and `web/messages/en/index.ts` (one import and one key per new namespace, alphabetical), `shared/src/index.ts` and `shared/src/ai/index.ts` (exports), `shared/src/schemas/index.ts` after task 1 has merged. ## 12. Decisions taken by the planner

Each is a choice the issues left open; the owner can veto any by opening a `needs-owner` issue.

1. **One migration, in one lane, first.** Three features, one schema change (`0005_feel`), so wave 2's three lanes never queue behind a renumber. The lane also plumbs the new columns through every existing reader and writer, so the feature lanes find them in place.
2. **The symbol is stored on `Meal`, not derived at read time.** The issue asks that a library edit never rewrites a past day; a stored `symbol` is the only way to keep that true across the day, week and curve views without snapshotting the category on every item.
3. **Ten categories, named by what the child sees.** `starch` for pasta, rice and potato and `protein` for meat, fish and egg keep the enum short and the symbol unambiguous; the names are message keys, the enum values are English like every other enum.
4. **The symbol drawings are made in code, with a design sheet in the same PR.** #196 asked for the drawings in `docs/design/` first; there is no Claude Design round scheduled and the eleven icons are small. Task 5 writes `food-symbols.md` and task 7 asks the owner to accept or redraw.
5. **No curve in simpleMode in v0.6.0.** The simple Now has no chart today; the symbol goes on the last-meal card. A chart for the child is its own decision.
6. **A symptom is loggable by everyone who can log a meal, readable by everyone.** A viewer reads symptoms as it reads meals, because a symptom carries no glucose; the detail view withholds the surrounding readings for a viewer.
7. **Custom symptom kinds have a data model and an API, no editor.** `Space.symptomKinds` and `custom:<slug>` exist so the shape is right from the first migration; the Settings editor is a later task (task 7).
8. **The push goes to carers only when a simpleMode member logs.** That mirrors "Spørg en voksen": a parent at work hears from the child, not from the other parent.
9. **The recipe form has its own microphone; the Say sheet stays a meal parser.** Dictating ingredients into the recipe's text field reuses `MicButton` and keeps the meal prompt small; the Say sheet does learn symptoms (task 3), because a feeling said at the table belongs there.
10. **JSON-LD before the model, always.** A `schema.org/Recipe` page is read without an AI call so the common case costs nothing and works when AI is off.
11. **The share target lands in `/api/share` and redirects.** The locale middleware would redirect a `POST` to a page; an API route stores the share for ten minutes in process memory (one web instance on Static Web Apps; a lost share is a retry, not data loss) and redirects to the form.
12. **Ingredient grams by a small unit table; `stk` from the matched food's first portion.** Good enough for carbs; the person corrects any line before saving, and the number is theirs after the save.
13. **The enrich-foods timer fills categories as a third pass under the normalise cap.** No new timer, no new secret; the category comes from the small role.
14. **Symptom markers are diamonds in the text colour.** The design rule reserves terracotta for meals; a symptom is information, not an alarm, so it takes neither the meal colour nor red.
15. **The `needs-owner` label on #194 is cleared by this plan.** The triage agent asked for the owner's decision on a new kind of entry; the owner created milestone `v0.6.0` for it the same day.
16. **"Edit a meal after it's logged" is reachability, not a new editor.** The v0.3.0 sheet already edits everything a meal has; what the owner's phone lacks is a way to it from the rows and a visible button on detail. Task 8 adds those and changes no rule about who edits.
